ChatGPT & your data

# Does ChatGPT train on your data?

Short answer: on the personal plans (Free, Go, Plus and Pro), yes, unless you turn it off. Temporary Chat, Business, Enterprise and the API aren’t used by default.

Which plans

## Who gets trained on.

**Free, Go, Plus, Pro** Yes, by default. You can turn it off.

**Temporary Chat** No. And it isn’t kept in your history.

**Business, Enterprise, API** No, not by default.

Sources: [OpenAI Help Center, Data Controls](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8983077) · [how your data is used to improve model performance](https://openai.com/policies/how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance/) (updated 13 March 2026). Checked 23 October 2026.

Turn it off

## Three steps. Takes a minute.

1. **Open Settings** Click your profile picture, then Settings.
2. **Data Controls** Go to the Data Controls section.
3. **Switch it off** Turn off “Improve the model for everyone”.

It stops future chats from being used for training. It doesn’t delete what was already used, and your chats are still stored on OpenAI’s servers.

## What the switch *actually does.*

On: your chats can be used to train ChatGPT. Interactive, on the page: https://greencube.app/does-chatgpt-train-on-your-data#try-it

Who else

## Who can see your chats?

### People can read them

OpenAI says authorised staff and outside contractors can access conversations, for example for support, abuse checks and legal reasons.

### Deleted isn’t always deleted

In 2025, during the New York Times lawsuit, a court ordered OpenAI to keep chats people had deleted. That mostly ended in October 2025, but chats saved before then can still be kept.

### It’s not just OpenAI

Gemini, Claude, Copilot and Meta AI all have their own rules and switches. The shape is the same: if the AI runs on someone else’s computer, your words go there.

Sources: [OpenAI privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/row-privacy-policy/) · [the October 2025 order](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Oct/23/openai-no-longer-has-to-preserve).

Questions

## Quick answers.

### Does ChatGPT train on my chats?

On Free, Go, Plus and Pro, yes by default, unless you turn off “Improve the model for everyone” in Settings, Data Controls. Business, Enterprise and the API are not trained on by default.

### Does Temporary Chat get used for training?

No. Temporary Chats aren’t used for training, don’t appear in your history, and are deleted from OpenAI’s systems after 30 days.

### Does ChatGPT Plus train on your data?

Yes, by default. Plus is a personal plan like Free, Go and Pro, so paying doesn’t switch training off: turn off “Improve the model for everyone” in Settings, Data Controls.

### Is feedback used even with training off?

Yes. If you give a thumbs up or down, OpenAI says the whole conversation linked to that feedback may be used to train its models, even if you’ve opted out.

### Are deleted ChatGPT chats really deleted?

Usually, yes. But in 2025 a court ordered OpenAI to keep deleted chats; that mostly ended in October 2025, and chats saved before then can still be kept.
