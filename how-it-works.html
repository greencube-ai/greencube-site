Where does the AI actually live?

This is the only thing you really need to understand. Everything else on this page follows from it.

Every other AI

It lives in their building.

Your question crosses the internet, lands on their servers, and is stored there — where it can be kept, read, trained on, and cooled with real fresh water.

GreenCube

It lives inside your laptop.

The AI itself sits on your machine. Your words go from your keyboard to the model and back — never crossing the internet. There is no server to store them on.
