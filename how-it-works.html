Where does the AI actually live?

This is the only thing you really need to understand. Everything else on this page follows from it.

Every other AI

The AI lives in their building.

It sits in a huge building full of computers that they own, pay the power bill for, and cool with water. It can't answer you until your words get there — so everything you type travels across the internet, lands on their computers, and stays.

GreenCube

The AI lives on your computer.

It sits on the computer you already own, running off the same plug. No warehouse. No server. Your words go to it and come straight back, without ever leaving your machine.

What that gets you

Four things change once the AI is on your computer.

These aren't features we added. They just happen on their own, because the AI is sitting on your computer.

Nobody can read it

There's no copy on any server. So there's no worker, no reviewer and no court order that can reach your chats. Not because we promise to be good — because there's nothing to reach.

It's not a promise. There's just nothing there.

It never runs out

No credits. No daily limit. No "you've run out for today" in the middle of something. It's your own computer doing the work, and you already paid for that.

Ask all day. Nothing is counting.

You own it

Pay once and it's yours. You're not renting it, so nobody can take it away the day you stop paying — or the day they change the rules.

Owned, not rented.

No reservoir cools it

Their computers get so hot that fresh water is boiled away to cool them — about half a litre for every 20 to 50 questions. Your laptop just uses a fan.

No cooling tower. No reservoir.
