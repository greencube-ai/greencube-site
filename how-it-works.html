What cloud AI really costs

Trained on you

Your chats become their product

On every consumer plan, your conversations are used to train the model by default — unless you find the buried opt-out. Staff and contractors can read them, and a U.S. court has ordered chats preserved even after you delete them.

OpenAI policy · court order

½ litre

Water, evaporated — per short chat

Asking a cloud AI 20–50 questions evaporates about half a litre of fresh water to cool the servers. Training a single model, GPT‑3, drank roughly 700,000 litres — and Google's U.S. data centres used 12.7 billion litres in one year.

UC Riverside study

2× by 2030

A power drain the size of a country

Data centres already burn about 1.5% of the world's electricity, and the IEA expects that to roughly double by 2030 — much of it driven by AI. Every prompt spins up a machine in a warehouse you'll never see.
