How it works

It thinks. It does.

Every GreenCube task has two halves: a brain and a pair of hands.

The brain reads what you wrote, breaks it into steps, and picks the right app for each one.

The hands open those apps and do the work — click by click, the same way you would.

You write one sentence. It handles the rest.

Thinks
  • ReadsUnderstands what you actually want.
  • PlansBreaks the task into small steps.
  • ChecksWatches its own work for mistakes.
Does
  • OpensYour apps, browsers, files.
  • ActsClicks buttons, types, sends emails.
  • FinishesHands the done work back to you.

Whatever needs to happen — research, writing, code, errands — both halves work together until it's done.
