In their own words

Three things they tell you, quietly.

01

By default, they train on you.

On ChatGPT's consumer plans — Free, Plus, Go, Pro — your conversations train the model unless you switch it off. It isn't opt-in. It's opt-out, and the default favours them.

Source: OpenAI documentation

02

People can read them.

Not "an algorithm." People. OpenAI states that authorized employees and third-party contractors may access your conversations. Whatever you typed at 2am sits on a server, attached to your email, readable.

Source: OpenAI privacy policy

03

Deleted doesn't mean gone.

A court ordered OpenAI to preserve chat logs — including conversations people had already deleted. Once your words are on someone else's computer, deletion is a policy, not a fact.

Source: OpenAI on the NYT data demands

Questions

Asked before you get started.

?

Does cloud AI really train on my conversations?

On ChatGPT's consumer plans (Free, Plus, Go, Pro), yes — by default, unless you find the opt-out in settings. Business plans and the API are not trained on by default.

?

Can people actually read my chats?

Yes. OpenAI states authorized employees and specialized contractors may access conversations for engineering support, abuse investigation and legal compliance.

?

If I delete a chat, is it gone?

Not necessarily. A court ordered OpenAI to preserve chat logs that would otherwise have been deleted — including chats users deleted themselves. On someone else's server, deletion is a policy, not a fact.

How is GreenCube different?
