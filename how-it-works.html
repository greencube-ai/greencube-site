Why GreenCube

Three things, one machine.

A memory that grows with you. Agents that team up when a task gets big. Reach into the apps already on your laptop. All running locally — no cloud, no telemetry, nothing leaving your hard drive.

Memory

It actually remembers.

Cloud assistants forget you between chats. GreenCube doesn't. It builds a knowledge graph of who you are over weeks and months — your projects, your tics, your taste, your people. The longer you use it, the sharper it gets.

An AI that knows you should be one only you control. Your graph stays on your hard drive. One click — and it's gone forever.

Team

One AI. An entire team.

When a task is complex, GreenCube spawns specialized agents that work together — like having a startup team inside your laptop. They share findings, hand off work, and ship results. All running locally. All free.

Researcher Browses, reads papers, finds answers
Engineer Writes, debugs, ships code in your terminal
Writer Drafts docs, emails, prose in your voice
Operator Uses your apps, manages files, automates
Tools

It doesn't just talk. It does.

Most AI assistants are trapped in a chat window. GreenCube lives in your operating system. It opens your terminal, browses the web, reads and edits your files, and uses the apps you already have. All locally — your machine, your data, your tools.
