What "offline" really means

An offline chatbot runs the AI model on your own device. Your messages are processed by your computer or phone instead of a company's server, which is why it keeps working on a plane or with the Wi-Fi off.

That doesn't mean it never touches the internet. The model is a file of a few gigabytes, so the first download needs a connection; after that it stays on your device. Some features also go online when you use them: update checks, searching for new models, cloud models and web search. In most of these apps those parts are optional and you can stay fully local.

And "local" doesn't always mean the app collects nothing. Your chats stay on your device, but some apps send anonymous usage data about the app itself, which is what the next table is about.

Which one fits you?

You're on Windows and don't want to choose models

This is the person I built GreenCube for. It looks at your graphics card and memory and downloads the AI that fits, so there's nothing to pick. It reads PDFs and photos, makes flashcards and study guides, and generates pictures, for €9.99 once. If you'd rather not pay and don't mind choosing a model from a list, LM Studio, Jan and GPT4All are free desktop apps. There's more on what a Windows PC needs.

You're on a Mac

LM Studio (Apple chips only, macOS 14+), Jan, Ollama, GPT4All, Msty and Atomic Chat all have Mac versions. GreenCube is Windows only.

You want it on your phone

PocketPal AI and Atomic Chat run models on the phone itself, on both iPhone and Android, and AnythingLLM has an Android app. A phone fits smaller models than a gaming PC, so expect simpler answers.

You're a developer, or you want a server

Ollama and llama.cpp are command-line engines with a local API, and KoboldCpp is a single .exe with a browser chat. For a shared chat page that several people use, Open WebUI or AnythingLLM's Docker version sit on top of an engine.

What an offline chatbot can't do

It doesn't know today's news. A model only knows what it learned before it was released, unless the app can search the web, and then that part is online.

It's also smaller than ChatGPT or Gemini. Models that fit on a normal PC handle everyday and study questions well, but the biggest cloud AIs are still better at the hardest problems. All of them make mistakes, so check the facts that matter.

And your device does the work. On an 8 GB laptop it writes about as fast as you read; on a gaming PC it's far quicker. Either way it uses your battery and memory while it writes. We re-read every app's pages each month and update the date at the top when something changes.
