None of the big cloud AIs sells a one-time purchase. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity all bill you every month (or every year). If you want no monthly fee, there are three real routes: a free plan with its limits, a student offer, or an AI that runs on your own computer, which you either buy once or get for free.

The three real ways to avoid a monthly fee

A free plan, with its limits

Free ChatGPT text chats are no longer limited, but files, pictures and voice are, and it's starting to show ads in some countries (we checked the details). Free Gemini allows up to 10 files per message, with limits that refresh every 5 hours.

That's fine for quick questions. For exam prep with your own notes, you'll feel the limits. Most free plans also train on what you type unless you switch it off (we checked 9).

A student offer

If you're at university and 18 or over, Google offers Google AI Plus free for a year, redeemable until 31 December 2026 (Google). It asks for a payment method when you sign up, and if you don't cancel in time it then charges €4.99 a month. If it fits you, it's a good deal, so take it and put the cancel date in your calendar.

An AI that runs on your own PC

Here the model is a file on your computer, so there's no server to pay for every month and nothing counting your messages. GreenCube is €9.99 once and picks the AI that fits your PC by itself. There are also free apps for running AI on your computer, like LM Studio, Jan or GPT4All, where you choose and download the models yourself. They all share the same cost: your computer does the work.

Which suits you?

  • If you use AI now and then, a free plan is enough.
  • If you're at university and want more than the free plan this year, check Google's AI Plus offer before paying for anything, and note when to cancel.
  • If you use it every day, with your own notes, offline, or without uploading anything, an AI on your PC is cheaper from the first month and has no usage limits.
  • If you need the strongest AI for very hard problems, the biggest cloud AIs are still better at that, and they bill monthly.
