The smallest Gemma 4, E2B, runs on an 8 GB laptop with no graphics card. E4B wants 16 GB of RAM, or any graphics card with 6 GB or more, where it's fast. Gemma 4 12B needs at least 10 GB of VRAM to hold a real conversation.

These numbers come from our own tests. GreenCube, the app I build, runs on Gemma 4 (Cube 2B is built on Gemma 4 E2B and Cube 4B on Gemma 4 E4B), so we've timed these models on {{pcs}} real PCs, in September and October 2026. Gemma 4 itself is free: Google released it under the Apache 2.0 licence, which allows commercial use.

What we learned

E2B is the laptop model

On a 16 GB laptop with no graphics card, E2B finished a full study answer in about 20 seconds. E4B took 112 seconds for the same thing. E2B also got all five of our quick fact checks right. The full comparison is in E2B vs E4B, measured.

E4B is the gaming PC model

Once the whole model sits on a graphics card, E4B is 7 to 9 times faster than on a laptop, and its answers are better than E2B's. Any card with 6 GB or more holds it completely.

12B needs real VRAM, not just RAM

On an 8 GB card, 12B answered one short question fine, then crashed every time the chat grew. That PC had 48 GB of RAM, and it didn't help, because the model and the conversation both have to fit on the card. There's more on this in why the model must fit on your card.
