What we learned

1. E2B is the laptop model

On a 16 GB laptop with no graphics card it finished a full study answer in about 20 seconds, where E4B took 112. In five quick fact checks it got none wrong. E2B vs E4B, measured.

2. E4B is the gaming PC model

Once the whole model sits on a graphics card, E4B is 7 to 9 times faster than on a laptop, and its answers are better. Any 6 GB+ card does it.

3. 12B needs real VRAM, not just RAM

On an 8 GB card, 12B answered one short question fine, then crashed every time the chat grew. That PC had 48 GB of RAM and it didn't help. Why the model must fit on your card.
