What changed in August 2026

In the week of 10 August 2026, OpenAI made text chats unlimited on its Free and Go plans (Engadget). Its own help page now says: "Free users have unlimited everyday text chats, subject to abuse-prevention safeguards. File uploads, image generation, voice, data analysis, and other tools have separate usage limits." (OpenAI Help Center).

So if you saw "You've reached your limit" in 2025 while just chatting, that's mostly gone. If you see it in 2026, it's almost always because you sent a file, asked for a picture, or used voice.

Why the caps are where they are

Reading a 40-page PDF or drawing a picture costs a cloud AI far more computing than a text reply, and on the Free plan someone has to pay for those servers. OpenAI itself says keeping the Free and Go plans running "requires significant infrastructure", which is the reason it gives for the ads (OpenAI). An AI running on your own PC doesn't have that problem: your graphics card does the work, so there is nothing to ration. The cost is that it's only as fast as your PC (we measured 7 PCs here).

So what should you use?

Stay on ChatGPT Free if…

you mostly chat and rarely upload files. Unlimited text covers most questions, and its models are stronger than anything that fits on a PC.

Pay for Plus if…

you need the strongest models, voice, or deep research every day, and $20 a month is fine for you.

Use an AI on your PC if…

you study from PDFs and slides a lot, want pictures without a counter, often have bad Wi-Fi, or don't want another subscription.

Or mix them

Lots of people use ChatGPT for quick questions and an AI on their own PC for the heavy, repetitive stuff like summarising a whole semester of PDFs.

Exam-season tip

Caps hurt most right before exams, when you want to turn every PDF into a summary and flashcards in one evening. Whatever you use, do the heavy stuff early in the week, and keep one tool with no counter for the night before.
