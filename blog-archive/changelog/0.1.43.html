The app got easier on the eyes, and much better at knowing where each message should go.

What's new

  • Easier to read: bigger text, more space between lines, clear headings, and tables and quotes in the soft colours of greencube.app.
  • One chat for everything. Study guides, notes and coding help come from the same chat, in the language you asked in.
  • Online answers for almost anything: people, places, history, science, news, prices and weather, plus football, F1 and NBA results from sports data.
  • Change a picture. Attach one and describe what it should become: the same scene at night, as a watercolour, in winter.

What we fixed

"Dinner idea" opened the picture maker

When GreenCube wasn't sure where a message should go, the engine guessed, and "3 bullet points on sleep", "translate to English" and "imagine you are a pirate" all went to Images. A small model that understands meaning now decides most messages.

197 messages it had never seen, sent to the right place (E2B): 69% → 94%.

"When did the Berlin Wall fall?" found a page on a medical condition

Search sites sometimes return nonsense: "What is CRISPR?" brought back cheap holidays, and "¿A qué temperatura hierve el agua?" found a place called Hierve el Agua. Results that share too little with your question are now dropped.

Right answer reaching the engine: 65% → 85%.

Sports through web search were slow and mostly wrong

They now come from sports data.

10 sports questions: 3 answered in 17–172 seconds → 10 answered in 0.2–4.2 seconds.

The second message of every chat started from scratch

We found it in a friend's log on a GTX 1650 PC. The same answers now need far less reading.

Second message (E2B): 39.9 seconds → 11.0 seconds.
