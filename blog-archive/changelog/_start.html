How it started

  • 25 March 2026: the first line of code.
  • 4 July 2026: version 0.1.1, the first one delivered by the app's own updater. Then 0.1.2 to 0.1.15 in five days.
  • 19 September 2026: 0.1.39, the Code tab, and the "What's new" card you see after every update.

Numbers on these pages were measured on our test laptop unless they say otherwise: an Intel Core Ultra 5 with 16 GB of memory and no graphics card. E2B and E4B are the two Gemma 4 engines GreenCube runs on your PC, one small and quick, one bigger.
