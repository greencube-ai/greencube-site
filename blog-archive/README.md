# Blog archive (BabyLoveGrowth, saved 2026-10-10)

A full copy of blog.greencube.app, taken before BabyLoveGrowth goes offline (~2026-11-04).
Not part of the website: `.vercelignore` keeps this folder out of every deploy.

- `index.json`: one entry per post (87): slug, old URL, title, meta description, dates,
  cover image, FAQ, word count, and the wrong facts found by `tools/flag.py`.
- `posts/<slug>.html`: the article body only (BabyLoveGrowth's "Visit GreenCube" cards removed),
  with image links pointing at `images/`.
- `images/`: every image the posts used (263), downloaded from BabyLoveGrowth's storage.
- `raw-pages-2026-10-10.tar.gz`: the untouched pages as served, plus the blog sitemap.
- `tools/`: the scripts that made this (`extract.py raw/ out/`, `flag.py out/`).

The posts were written by BabyLoveGrowth and many describe the old product
(€8.99/€9, "Quick" and "All-rounder" models, a 14-day refund). Fix them before republishing.
