Questions people ask before buying.

Is it really one payment?

Yes. €9.99 once and it’s yours. No subscription, no renewal, no second charge.

Will it run on my PC?

If it runs Windows 10 or 11 and has 8 GB of RAM or more, yes. A graphics card makes it fast; without one it’s slower, but it works. Check your PC in two questions.

Do I need internet?

Once, to sign in and download the AI. After that it works with no internet: on a plane, in a blackout, anywhere. It only goes online for updates, or if you switch Online on.

Does it really save water?

Yes. Cloud AI runs in data centres that get hot, and many cool down by evaporating fresh water. GreenCube runs on your own PC, which cools itself with a fan, so your questions use no data-centre water. The app shows an estimate of the water you’ve saved. Your PC still uses electricity, which has its own footprint. How we count.

Why do I need an account?

Only to check that you paid. It never sees your chats, files or images.

Is it as smart as ChatGPT?

No. The biggest cloud AIs are smarter. GreenCube runs on your PC, works offline and is yours. That’s the trade.

What if it’s not for me?

You have 15 days to change your mind. Email support@greencube.app and we’ll refund you in full.
