Questions

Quick answers.

No. There’s no server that has them, so there’s nothing on our side to read.

Only to check that you paid. It’s your email and nothing else.

Can I delete everything?

Yes. Delete chats and memories in the app. It’s all on your PC, so it’s yours to delete.

Does it work with no internet?

Yes. After signing in and downloading the AI once, it works fully offline.
