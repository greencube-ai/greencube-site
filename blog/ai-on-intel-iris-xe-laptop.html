Yes. On a laptop with no graphics card (Intel processor with Iris Xe graphics) and 8 GB of RAM, Gemma 4 E2B wrote 9.3 tokens a second. You read about 5, so answers come out faster than you can read them.

What it means

No graphics card? It still works

With no card, the AI runs on the processor. The smaller Gemma 4 E2B wrote 9.3 tokens a second. Running it on the built-in Intel graphics was slower here (5), so the processor is the better place.

Pictures

No. The picture AI needs about 7 GB of memory to itself, more than an 8 GB laptop can free up. Chat and study work fine.

The test PC

  • Laptop
  • Graphics: built-in Intel graphics only
  • Graphics memory (VRAM): none (no separate graphics card)
  • RAM: 8 GB
  • Processor: Intel, 8 threads
