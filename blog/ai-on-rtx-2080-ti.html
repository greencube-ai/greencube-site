Can an RTX 2080 Ti run AI? Measured: 65–71 tokens a second

By Hector Gras · 14 October 2026 · Leer en español

Yes. On a desktop with an NVIDIA RTX 2080 Ti (11 GB) and 128 GB of RAM, Gemma 4 E4B wrote 65–71 tokens a second. You read about 5, so answers come out faster than you can read them.

What it means

The card is 5.6× faster than the processor

Same PC, same AI (Gemma 4 E4B): 13.5 tokens a second on the processor alone, 75.6 on the graphics card. AI is the kind of maths graphics cards are built for.

The whole AI fits on the card

Gemma 4 E4B is a 4.2 GB file and the card has 11 GB of its own memory (VRAM), so all of it runs on the card. That is what makes it this fast.

Room for Gemma 4 12B

With 11 GB, the bigger Gemma 4 12B (6.4 GB) should fit on this card too. We haven't measured it here yet.

Pictures

Yes. One picture (512×512) took 8–9 s on the graphics card (the first took 83 s while it loaded).

The test PC

  • Desktop
  • Graphics: NVIDIA RTX 2080 Ti

  • RAM: 128 GB
  • Processor: 32 threads
