Can a GTX 1650 run AI? Measured: about 20 tokens a second

By Hector Gras · 23 September 2026 · Leer en español

Yes. On a desktop with an NVIDIA GTX 1650 (4 GB) and 16 GB of RAM, Gemma 4 E4B wrote about 20 tokens a second. You read about 5, so answers come out faster than you can read them.

What it means

Not all of it fits on the card

Gemma 4 E4B is a 4.2 GB file and the card has 4 GB, so it does not fit whole and part of it sits in the normal memory (RAM). It still writes about 20 tokens a second. Reading what you paste swung between 24 and 395 tokens a second from one day to the next, most likely because the card was full.

Gemma 4 12B needs a bigger card

GreenCube suggests the bigger Gemma 4 12B only on cards with 10 GB or more. On an 8 GB AMD RX 5700 XT it answered short questions, then crashed in a long chat.
