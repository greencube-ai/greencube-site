Keep Chats Local on Windows: ChatGPT or GreenCube (€9.99 One-Time)

By Hector Gras · 28 August 2026

We checked this
  • Which AI doesn't train on your chats? We checked 9 (October 2026)
  • AI without a subscription: what actually exists in 2026, with checked prices

ChatGPT is the stronger AI: it reasons better on hard problems, searches the web and works on every device you own. GreenCube is the better fit if you want your chats kept on your own Windows PC, an AI that works without internet, and one payment of €9.99 instead of a monthly bill. Plenty of people end up using both.

I build GreenCube, so weigh my opinion with that in mind. I've tried to be fair to ChatGPT, because it's a very good product, and the right answer depends on what you use AI for.

The real difference: where the AI runs

When you type into ChatGPT, your message goes to OpenAI's servers, a very large model writes the answer there, and the conversation is saved to your account. That's why it works the same on your phone, your laptop and a library computer, and why it needs a connection every time.

GreenCube downloads a smaller AI onto your PC the first time you open it. After that, the answers are written by your own computer, and your chats are saved on it. There's nothing on our side to read, because we never receive them.

Where ChatGPT is better

Raw ability. The models behind ChatGPT are far bigger than anything that fits in a laptop's memory, and it shows on long chains of reasoning, hard code and obscure facts. It also searches the web, so it can tell you what happened this morning, and it copes with much longer documents in one go.

It's also everywhere. You can start a chat on your phone on the bus and finish it on a Mac at home, and there are plans for teams who want shared workspaces. GreenCube does none of that: it lives on one Windows PC, and there's no Mac or phone version.

Where GreenCube is the better fit

The first reason is privacy you don't have to set up. OpenAI lets you turn off training on your chats and use temporary chats (more in is ChatGPT private?), but your conversations still travel to its servers and are stored there. With GreenCube, the chats, the files you drop in and the images it makes stay on your PC. You sign in once, with Google or Microsoft, only so it can check that you paid.

It also works with no connection. Once it's set up, you can use it on a plane, on a train with patchy signal, or with the Wi-Fi switched off on purpose. It only goes online for updates, or if you turn on Online for web lookups.

Nothing runs out. There's no message cap, no credits and no monthly fee, so making forty sets of flashcards the night before an exam costs the same as making one. ChatGPT's free plan has limits that change over time; we keep track of them in ChatGPT's free limits.

And it makes things, not only answers. GreenCube turns a request into a slide deck you can save as PowerPoint, a document, a quiz, flashcards, a labelled diagram or a picture, and it reads PDFs, Word files and photos. All of that happens on your machine.

What your PC needs

ChatGPT runs on anything with a browser. GreenCube needs a Windows 10 or 11 PC with about 8 GB of memory or more. It checks your PC and picks the model for you: Cube 2B on smaller PCs, Cube 4B on PCs with a graphics card, both built on Google's Gemma. Who should pick which

Pick ChatGPT if you need the strongest model for hard work, like research across many sources, serious coding or very long documents. It's also the right choice if you want web search and current news built in, or if you mostly use AI on a phone or a Mac.

Pick GreenCube if you work on a Windows PC and want your chats kept on it, if you're often without a reliable connection, if you're tired of hitting limits, or if you'd rather pay €9.99 once than $20 a month. It suits studying, writing, everyday questions and making decks, quizzes and pictures.

Or use both: ChatGPT when you're online and the problem is hard, and GreenCube for private notes, offline work and anything you'd rather not upload. If you're a student or work with client files, there's a version of this comparison written for you in ChatGPT vs GreenCube for students and professionals.

Questions

Is GreenCube really free from cloud servers?

Your chats are: your own PC writes the answers and saves them, so there's nothing on our side to read. It goes online only to sign you in once (to check you paid), to download the AI, for updates, and for web lookups if you switch Online on.

Which GreenCube model should I pick?

You don't: GreenCube checks your PC and uses Cube 2B on smaller PCs or Cube 4B on PCs with a graphics card, both built on Google's Gemma.

Does GreenCube cost more than ChatGPT over time?

No. GreenCube is EUR 9.99 (US$9.99) once, less than one month of ChatGPT Plus at $20. ChatGPT also has a free plan with limits, and its paid plans get you a much stronger model.

Can GreenCube read images or only text?

Both. It reads photos, PDFs and Word files on your PC, and it can make pictures too, which needs 12 GB of memory.

What happens if GreenCube doesn't work on my laptop?

Email support@greencube.app within 15 days and you get a full refund. It needs Windows 10 or 11 and about 8 GB of memory, and on an 8 GB laptop with no graphics card it writes at about reading speed.
