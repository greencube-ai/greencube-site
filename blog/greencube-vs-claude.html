Save $190+ a Year Choosing GreenCube vs Claude for Private Offline AI

By Hector Gras · 31 August 2026

Over a year, Claude Pro costs $204 to $240 ($20 a month, or $17 a month if you pay yearly), while GreenCube costs €9.99 (US$9.99) once. That saving is real, but you aren't buying the same thing: Claude is a much stronger AI with web search and apps everywhere, and GreenCube is a smaller AI that runs on your own Windows PC, offline, with your chats kept there.

I build GreenCube, so weigh my opinion with that in mind. This post is about money: what each option costs over time, what the subscription actually pays for, and the costs that don't show up on a price tag. For a feature and privacy comparison, see Claude or GreenCube for privacy.

What the subscription pays for

It would be unfair to compare only prices. $20 a month buys access to some of the strongest AI models available, running on hardware no laptop can match. In practice that means better answers on hard problems, code that works across a whole project, and the ability to read very long documents at once.

It also pays for things a cloud service does well: web search for current information, Projects to keep files and instructions together, apps on Windows, Mac, iPhone and Android, Claude Code for developers, and higher usage limits than the free plan. If you use those every day, the subscription is good value and GreenCube won't replace it.

What the one payment pays for

GreenCube is a different deal. For €9.99 you get an AI that lives on your PC and keeps working with the Wi-Fi off. Your chats, files and images are saved on your computer, and there are no message limits, which matters because even Claude's paid plans have usage limits (see how Claude's limits work).

Besides chatting, it makes slide decks that save as PowerPoint, documents, quizzes, flashcards, labelled diagrams, pictures and small web pages, and it reads PDFs, Word files and photos. It's in 19 languages. You need internet once, to sign in and download the AI, and after that only for updates or if you switch on Online for web lookups.

Costs that aren't on the price tag

A local AI is only cheap if you already own a PC that can run it. GreenCube needs Windows 10 or 11 and about 8 GB of memory, and speed depends on the machine. On the PCs we tested, an 8 GB laptop with no graphics card wrote at 5 to 7 tokens a second (about reading speed), a 16 GB laptop at 14 to 18, and most gaming PCs with a 6 GB or bigger graphics card at 37 to 76. Pictures need 12 GB of memory. If you'd have to buy new hardware, the saving disappears. Check your PC first.

There's also your time. A smaller model is weaker on the hardest questions, so a tough problem may take more back and forth, or need a cloud model anyway. On the Claude side, the hidden cost is that the bill never stops, and everything depends on a connection.

Which way of paying suits you

If you use AI for hard work every day, coding or long research for instance, pay for Claude. The subscription is worth it, and nothing on a laptop will match it on those tasks.

If you use AI a few times a week for study, drafts and everyday questions, paying $240 a year is a lot for that. GreenCube covers most of it for €9.99, with the bonus that your chats stay on your PC and it works offline.

A cheap combination works well too: Claude's free plan for the occasional hard question, and GreenCube for everything private, everything offline, and as much as you like.

Questions

Is GreenCube Really Free of Cloud Dependency?

Almost. It needs the internet once, to sign in with Google or Microsoft (only to check you paid) and to download the AI. After that it works offline, going online only for updates or if you switch Online on.

Can GreenCube Read Images Like Claude Can?

Yes. GreenCube reads photos, PDFs and Word files on your PC without uploading them. Claude is stronger on long or complicated documents, since it can read very long ones at once.

How Much Does GreenCube Cost Compared to Claude's Subscription?

GreenCube costs €9.99 (US$9.99) once, with a 15-day refund. Claude Pro is $20 a month, or $17 a month paid yearly, which comes to $204 to $240 a year.

Does GreenCube Work Without Internet Access?

Yes, after setup. Once you've signed in and the AI has downloaded, chats and reading documents work with no internet connection.

Which Is Better for Coding and Complex Research, GreenCube or Claude?

Claude. It's a much stronger model, running on hardware no laptop can match, so it handles hard code, whole projects and long research better. GreenCube suits study, drafts and everyday questions you want to keep on your PC.
