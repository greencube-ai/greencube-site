ChatGPT vs GreenCube: Offline Privacy for Students and Professionals

By Hector Gras · 24 September 2026

We checked this
  • Which AI doesn't train on your chats? We checked 9 (October 2026)

For students and professionals, the choice comes down to what you put into the AI. ChatGPT is smarter and searches the web, so it's better for hard research and anything current. GreenCube runs on your own Windows PC and works offline, so it's the better fit for lecture notes, drafts, client files and anything you'd rather not upload. It costs €9.99 once, against $20 a month for ChatGPT Plus.

I build GreenCube, so weigh my opinion with that in mind. This post looks at the comparison through the work students and professionals actually do. For the general version, see ChatGPT or GreenCube on Windows.

What changes when the AI runs on your PC

ChatGPT sends every message, and every file you attach, to OpenAI's servers. The answer is written there and the chat is kept in your account history until you delete it. On personal accounts, your chats can be used to improve OpenAI's models unless you switch that off in the settings; business plans come with different terms.

GreenCube does the work on your computer. You need internet once, to sign in with Google or Microsoft (only to check that you paid) and to download the AI on first launch. After that it works offline, and it only goes online for updates or if you switch on Online for web lookups. Your chats, files and images are saved on your PC.

For students

ChatGPT is the better tutor for the hardest material. If you're stuck on a long proof, need a tricky piece of code explained, or are writing an essay about something that happened last week, a big cloud model with web search will do a better job.

GreenCube is built for the everyday side of studying. You can drop in a lecture PDF and get flashcards, a quiz or a study guide from it, or turn your notes into a slide deck that saves as PowerPoint. There's no cap, so a long revision night doesn't end with "you've reached your limit", and it keeps working in a library with bad Wi-Fi. The price matters too: €9.99 once is less than one month of Plus.

One thing an offline app doesn't change is your school's rules. If your course limits AI use, those limits apply wherever the AI runs. What school AI policies usually say is worth a read.

For professionals

Client contracts, patient notes, HR files and unreleased work are the kind of material that employers and clients often don't want pasted into a cloud chatbot. With a cloud tool, you're relying on the provider's settings and contract; ChatGPT's business plans give stronger terms than a personal account, and many companies use them for exactly this reason.

With GreenCube, the files never leave the laptop, so there's less to check. To be clear, it isn't a compliance product and I won't claim it makes you compliant with any law. What it changes is simpler: the text isn't sent to us or anyone else. If rules like GDPR apply to you, this guide to GDPR and AI goes through the questions to ask.

ChatGPT still wins for some professional work: summarising very long reports in one go, sharing chats with a team, deep analysis, serious coding and anything that needs the live web.

Will your laptop run it?

A student laptop is often the weak point, so it's worth knowing before you buy. GreenCube needs Windows 10 or 11 and about 8 GB of memory. On an 8 GB laptop with no graphics card it writes at about reading speed (5 to 7 tokens a second on the PCs we tested); a 16 GB laptop is two or three times faster, and a gaming PC much faster again. It picks the right model for your machine on its own. You can check your PC here.

A habit that works with either

Keep sensitive things local by default, and send something to a cloud AI only when the task really needs it. When you do, take out names, account numbers and anything that identifies a client or a classmate first. That way you get the cloud's strength on hard problems without handing over more than you have to.

Who should pick which

Pick ChatGPT if your work leans on hard reasoning, current information or very long documents, if you share work with a team, or if you study on a phone or a Mac.

Pick GreenCube if you study or work on a Windows laptop, handle notes and files you'd rather keep to yourself, often work without a good connection, or want to stop paying every month. Many people will be best served by both: GreenCube for the private everyday work, ChatGPT for the occasional problem that needs the biggest model.

Questions

Is GreenCube really offline, or does it send data anywhere?

It works offline after setup, and your chats, files and images stay on your PC. It only goes online to sign in once with Google or Microsoft (to check you paid), to download the AI on first launch, for updates, and for web lookups if you switch Online on.

How much does GreenCube cost?

EUR 9.99 (US$9.99) once: no subscription, no message limits, everything included, and a 15-day refund.

Which GreenCube model will my PC use?

GreenCube picks for you: Cube 2B on smaller PCs, Cube 4B on PCs with a graphics card, both built on Google's Gemma. You need Windows 10 or 11 and about 8 GB of memory.

Is ChatGPT better than GreenCube for every task?

No, but it's better at the hardest ones: long reasoning, serious coding, very long documents and anything that needs the live web. GreenCube suits everyday studying, writing and work on files you'd rather keep on your PC.

Does GreenCube work on Mac and Windows?

Only Windows 10 and 11. There's no Mac or Linux version.
