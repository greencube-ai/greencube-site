Bard alternatives in 2026: Bard is now Gemini, so here's what else to use

By Hector Gras · 20 July 2026 · updated 24 October 2026

Bard hasn't existed under that name since 8 February 2024, when Google renamed it Gemini. If you liked Bard, Gemini is the same service. Outside Google, the main alternatives are ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot and Perplexity. If you'd rather your chats never left your computer, apps like LM Studio, Jan, Ollama or GreenCube run the AI offline on your own PC.

I build GreenCube, so weigh my opinion with that in mind. The rules for the other assistants come from their own help pages, and I've tried to stick to facts anyone can check.

What happened to Bard

Google launched Bard in 2023 as its answer to ChatGPT. On 8 February 2024 it renamed Bard to Gemini, after its family of AI models, and added mobile apps and a paid tier at the same time (Axios). So searching for "Bard" today really means Gemini. Google has also had a Gemini app for Windows since 10 September 2026 (our desktop apps check).

Free limits, if that's why you're switching

ChatGPT Free has had unlimited text chats since August 2026, but file uploads and pictures are capped (details). Free Gemini has limits that refresh every 5 hours up to a weekly cap, and takes up to 10 files per message, 100 MB each.

An AI on your own PC has no message, file or picture limits, because no server is counting them (which AI is really unlimited).

The offline option: AI that runs on your own computer

Every cloud assistant above, Gemini included, needs internet and sends your chats to the company. A local app downloads an AI model once and runs it on your own graphics card or processor. What you pay instead is speed, which depends on your PC. We measured GreenCube on 10 real PCs:

LM Studio, Jan and Ollama are free, and you pick the model. GreenCube (Windows, €9.99 once) picks the model for your PC and also reads PDFs and photos and makes pictures.

The trade-off is real. Models small enough for a PC are good for studying, writing and everyday questions, but the biggest cloud models are still stronger on the hardest problems, and an offline AI doesn't know today's news. You can compare the apps in 12 offline AI chatbots, checked, and see what your card can do in Can my PC run AI?

When Gemini is still the right pick

If you live in Gmail, Docs and Drive, Gemini can work with them directly, and it's the same assistant you used as Bard. Just check Keep Activity in your settings if you'd rather your chats weren't used for training.

Questions

Does Google Bard still exist?

Not under that name. Google renamed Bard to Gemini on 8 February 2024; it's the same assistant, now at gemini.google.com and in the Gemini apps.

What is the best alternative to Bard?

Gemini, since it's Bard under a new name. Outside Google, the main ones are ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot and Perplexity. For one that works offline and keeps your chats on your computer, try a local app like LM Studio, Jan, Ollama or GreenCube.

Which Bard alternative doesn't train on my chats?

On a free personal account, Claude trains only if you allow it, and Gemini only while Keep Activity is on. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Mistral Le Chat, DeepSeek and Meta AI train on what you type by default (checked October 2026). An AI on your own PC sends nothing anywhere.

Is there a free Bard alternative with no limits?

ChatGPT Free comes close: unlimited text chats since August 2026, though files and pictures are limited. Apps that run AI on your own PC have no usage limits at all, because no server counts your messages: LM Studio, Jan and Ollama are free, and GreenCube is €9.99 once.
