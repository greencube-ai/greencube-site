Unlimited AI: which has no limits on messages, files and pictures? (2026)

By Hector Gras · 5 September 2026 · updated 23 October 2026

For text chat, ChatGPT Free has been unlimited since August 2026. For files, PDFs, pictures and voice, every cloud AI we checked has a cap. The only AI with no usage limits at all is one that runs on your own PC, because there is no server counting what you do. The price of that is speed: your PC sets it.

This page is about usage limits, meaning how many messages, files and pictures you get, not content filters. I build GreenCube, one of the PC options below, so keep that in mind when I give an opinion.

Why the cloud has limits and your PC doesn't

Every message to a cloud AI runs on the company's servers, and reading a 40-page PDF or drawing a picture costs far more than a text reply. On a free plan somebody pays for that, so the heavy parts get rationed. OpenAI itself says keeping its free plans running "requires significant infrastructure" (OpenAI).

An AI on your own PC uses your graphics card or processor instead. There's nothing to ration, so nobody counts.

What "unlimited" costs on your own PC: time

We measured GreenCube on 10 real PCs in September and October 2026. A reader gets through about 5 tokens a second, so that's the speed to beat.

Which one should you use?

If you mostly chat, ChatGPT Free is fine now that text is unlimited, and its models are stronger than anything that fits on a PC.

If you study from PDFs and slides, that's where the cloud caps bite. An AI on your PC reads as many files as you give it, and if your PC has 12 GB of RAM it can make pictures without a counter too.

If you want free and unlimited, LM Studio, Jan, Ollama and GPT4All cost nothing and have no limits, but you choose and download the model yourself. GreenCube costs €9.99 once, picks the model for your PC and makes pictures. All of them are compared in 12 offline AI apps.

Questions

Is there an AI with no message limit?

Yes. ChatGPT Free has had unlimited everyday text chats since August 2026 (with abuse safeguards). Any AI on your own PC has no message limit either, because no server counts them: GreenCube, LM Studio, Jan, Ollama and GPT4All, for example.

Which AI has no limits on file uploads?

No cloud AI we checked. ChatGPT Free caps uploads without saying how many, Gemini allows up to 10 files per message with limits that refresh every 5 hours, and NotebookLM takes 50 sources per notebook. An AI on your own PC reads files from your disk, so the only limit is your PC's time.

Is there a free AI with no limits?

For text chat, ChatGPT Free is now unlimited. For everything, files included, free apps that run AI on your PC (LM Studio, Jan, Ollama, GPT4All) have no limits, but you pick and download the model yourself; GreenCube is €9.99 once and also makes pictures.

Which AI makes unlimited pictures?

One that runs on your own PC, since cloud image tools cap free pictures. In our tests GreenCube made a 512×512 picture in 8 to 89 seconds on gaming PCs and in about 2 to 2.5 minutes on a 16 GB laptop; it needs 12 GB of RAM.

What does unlimited AI on my PC cost me?

Time instead of money, because your PC does the work. In our tests an 8 GB laptop wrote 5 to 7 tokens a second (about reading speed), a 16 GB laptop 14 to 18, and most gaming PCs 37 to 76.
