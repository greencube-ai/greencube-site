Unlimited AI: which has no limits on messages, files and pictures? (2026)

By Hector Gras · 23 October 2026

Short answer: for text chat, ChatGPT Free has been unlimited since August 2026. For everything else, files, PDFs, pictures and voice, every cloud AI we checked has a cap. The only AI with no usage limits at all is one that runs on your own PC, because there is no server counting: free apps like LM Studio, Jan and Ollama, or GreenCube (€9.99 once), which also makes pictures. The price of "unlimited" is that your PC sets the speed.

Why the cloud has limits and your PC doesn't

Every message to a cloud AI runs on the company's servers, and reading a 40-page PDF or drawing a picture costs far more than a text reply. On a free plan someone pays for that, so the heavy parts get rationed; OpenAI itself says keeping its free plans running "requires significant infrastructure" (OpenAI). An AI on your own PC uses your graphics card or processor instead, so there is nothing to ration and nobody counting.

Questions

Is there an AI with no message limit?

Yes. Since August 2026 ChatGPT Free has unlimited everyday text chats, subject to abuse safeguards. Any AI that runs on your own PC also has no message limit, because there is no server counting them: GreenCube, LM Studio, Jan, Ollama and GPT4All, for example.

Which AI has no limits on file uploads?

None of the cloud AIs we checked: ChatGPT Free caps file uploads without publishing a number, Gemini allows up to 10 files per message with limits that refresh every 5 hours, and NotebookLM allows 50 sources per notebook. An AI on your own PC reads files from your disk, so the only limit is your PC's time.

Is there a free AI with no limits?

For text chat, ChatGPT Free is now unlimited. For everything, including files, the free apps that run AI on your PC (LM Studio, Jan, Ollama, GPT4All) have no usage limits; you choose and download the model yourself. GreenCube costs €9.99 once and also makes pictures.

Which AI makes unlimited pictures?

Cloud image generators cap free pictures. An AI on your own PC has no counter: GreenCube made a 512×512 picture in 8 to 89 seconds on gaming PCs and in about 2 to 2.5 minutes on a 16 GB laptop in our tests. It needs a graphics card or 16 GB of RAM.

What does unlimited AI on my PC cost me?

Time instead of money. Your PC does the work: in our tests an 8 GB laptop wrote 5 to 7 tokens a second (about reading speed), a 16 GB laptop 14 to 18, and most gaming PCs 37 to 76.
