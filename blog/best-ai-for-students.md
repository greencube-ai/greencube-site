# Best AI for students (2026): studying, notes and exams, tested

> The best AI for students depends on the job: ChatGPT Free for quick questions, NotebookLM for citing your notes, Google's free year of AI Plus for university students, and an AI on your own PC for unlimited PDFs, flashcards and offline study. Checked limits and measured times.

There isn't one best AI for students, there's a best one for each job. For quick questions, use ChatGPT Free, which has had unlimited text since August 2026. For studying from your own notes with citations, NotebookLM is free and shows where each answer comes from. At university, claim Google's free year of AI Plus. For lots of PDFs, flashcards, pictures, offline study or notes you'd rather not upload, an AI on your own PC does the job.

That last kind is what I build, GreenCube, so weigh my opinion with that in mind. The limits below come from each company's own pages, and the times are our own tests.

## The best AI for each part of studying

| You want to… | Best free pick | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ask quick questions, get explanations | ChatGPT Free: unlimited text chats | Trains on your chats by default; ads in some countries |
| Study from your notes, with citations | NotebookLM: 50 sources per notebook, cites each answer | Your notes go to Google; needs internet |
| Make flashcards and quizzes | NotebookLM (exports CSV), or an AI on your PC | AI cards contain mistakes, so check them |
| Read many long PDFs | An AI on your PC: no file limit | Speed depends on your PC |
| Make pictures and diagrams | An AI on your PC: no counter | Pictures need at least 12 GB of RAM; a graphics card makes them much faster |
| Study offline | Only an AI on your PC works with no connection | Doesn't know today's news |

The limits come from each company's own pages: [ChatGPT Free](https://blog.greencube.app/blog/chatgpt-free-limits), [NotebookLM](https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/16213268?hl=en), [Google AI Plus for students](https://gemini.google/students/).

## Free deals worth knowing

If you're at university and 18 or over, Google gives a year of AI Plus free, redeemable until 31 December 2026. It asks for a payment method and charges €4.99 a month after the year unless you cancel, so note the date ([Google](https://gemini.google/students/)).

It's also worth asking your school. Many universities give students an AI through their own accounts, and work and school accounts are usually left out of training by default on the big services. That makes them more private than a personal free account.

## Who sees your notes

On a free personal account, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Mistral, DeepSeek and Meta AI train on what you type by default. Gemini does while Keep Activity is on, NotebookLM doesn't unless you send feedback, and Claude only does if you allow it. All 9 are in [which AI doesn't train on your chats](https://blog.greencube.app/blog/ai-that-doesnt-train-on-your-data).

If your notes belong to someone else, like your school or a classmate, check whether you may upload them at all.

## Is it cheating?

Using AI to understand a topic, quiz yourself or get feedback on a draft is usually allowed. Handing in AI-written work as your own usually isn't. Rules differ by course, so check your academic integrity policy, and when in doubt, ask.

## Will it run on your laptop?

8 GB of RAM works for chat and study, slowly. 16 GB is comfortable, and a gaming PC is fast. You can check yours in about 30 seconds with [Can my PC run AI?](https://blog.greencube.app/blog/can-my-pc-run-ai#checker)

## FAQ

### What is the best free AI for students?

For quick questions, ChatGPT Free (unlimited text since August 2026). For studying from your own notes with citations, NotebookLM (free, 50 sources per notebook). If you're at university and 18 or over, Google offers a free year of AI Plus, redeemable until 31 December 2026.

### What is the best AI for studying from PDFs?

NotebookLM if uploading to Google is fine. If you study from many PDFs, don't want to upload them, or study offline, an AI on your own PC has no file limits: in our test GreenCube answered 11 of 12 questions about long documents right, about 15 seconds each, on a 16 GB laptop.

### What is the best AI for exam preparation?

One that turns your material into questions and flashcards you answer without looking. NotebookLM makes flashcards and quizzes; GreenCube made 18 flashcards from a 4,800-word chapter in about 5 minutes, 30 of 36 right with its bigger model. Check every card against your notes.

### Is using AI to study cheating?

It depends on your school's rules. Using AI to understand material, test yourself and get feedback is usually fine; handing in AI-written work as your own usually isn't. Check your course's academic integrity policy.

### Which AI doesn't train on students' notes?

NotebookLM says it doesn't train on uploads unless you send feedback, and Claude only if you allow it. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Mistral, DeepSeek and Meta AI do by default on free personal accounts. An AI on your own PC never sends your notes anywhere.
