AI desktop apps for Windows: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot vs local apps (2026)

By Hector Gras · 4 August 2026 · updated 23 October 2026

AI desktop apps for Windows come in two kinds. Cloud apps such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot put the strongest models in a window, but they need internet and your chats go to the company. Local apps such as LM Studio, Jan, GPT4All and GreenCube run the AI on your own PC, so they work offline, have no usage limits and keep your chats on your machine, at whatever speed your PC manages.

I make GreenCube, one of the local apps, so take my opinion with that in mind. The newest arrival on the cloud side is Gemini, which Google launched for Windows on 10 September 2026.

The cloud apps win on raw ability, especially on the hardest questions. The local ones win on working anywhere and keeping your chats to yourself. On free personal accounts, ChatGPT and Perplexity train on your chats by default, which is worth knowing before you paste in anything private.

Plenty of people run one of each: a cloud app for the hardest questions, and a local one for offline study and notes they'd rather keep on their PC. If you're not sure what your PC can handle, Can my PC run AI? has the numbers for your card.

Questions

What is the best AI desktop app for Windows?

It depends on whether you want the strongest cloud model or an AI that works offline. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot all have Windows apps but need internet. LM Studio, Jan and GPT4All (free) and GreenCube (€9.99 once) run the AI on your own PC and work offline.

Is there a Gemini desktop app for Windows?

Yes. Google launched the Gemini app for Windows on 10 September 2026. It runs on Windows 10 or later with 8 GB of RAM and opens with Alt + Space; Google says some macOS features are still coming to Windows.

Do AI desktop apps work offline?

The cloud ones don't: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot are windows onto their companies' servers. Apps that run a model on your PC, like LM Studio, Jan, GPT4All and GreenCube, work offline after the first download.

Can the Perplexity desktop app run AI locally?

Perplexity says its Windows app has a local option for Pro and Max subscribers, which needs an NVIDIA GeForce RTX or RTX PRO card with at least 24 GB of VRAM.

Which AI desktop app keeps my chats on my PC?

Only apps that run the AI locally. In cloud desktop apps your chats go to the company's servers, and on free personal accounts ChatGPT and Perplexity train on them by default.
