LM Studio alternatives: 9 options for Windows, Mac, Linux and phones (2026)

By Hector Gras · 18 July 2026 · updated 23 October 2026

The right LM Studio alternative depends on why you're leaving. For open source, Jan is the closest swap (Apache 2.0, runs models itself, on Windows, Mac and Linux), and Ollama (MIT) is the other big one. On an Intel Mac, Ollama is the one whose docs still list Intel support. On a phone, try PocketPal AI or Atomic Chat.

I build GreenCube, one of the nine, so weigh my opinion with that in mind; I haven't ranked them. The facts come from each app's own website, docs or GitHub page, read on 21 October 2026, and on 23 October we re-checked Mac support on Jan's and Ollama's install pages. "Not stated" means an app's pages don't say. We haven't benchmarked these apps against each other.

Why people look for an LM Studio alternative

LM Studio is a good app. It's free, also for work, has a chat window and a local OpenAI-style server, and reads PDFs (LM Studio). The reasons to look elsewhere come from its own pages.

It's closed source, so if you want code you can read or build yourself, you need something else. It also has hardware limits: on Windows the processor needs AVX2, and on Mac it needs an Apple chip and macOS 14 or newer (requirements). That rules out older PCs and Intel Macs.

There's no phone app. And you choose the model yourself, which is freedom if you know models and a wall of names if you don't.

Pick by the reason you're leaving

You want open source

Jan is the closest swap: a desktop chat app that runs models itself, reads PDFs and Office files, and serves a local OpenAI-compatible API, under Apache 2.0 (GitHub). Ollama (MIT) is a command-line engine with a desktop chat window on Windows and Mac since July 2025 (Ollama). AnythingLLM (MIT) is built around chatting with your documents.

You have an Intel Mac

LM Studio and Jan both need an Apple chip (Jan). Ollama's macOS page lists Apple M-series and x86 (CPU only), on macOS 14 Sonoma or newer (Ollama docs). Expect it to be slow, because an Intel Mac runs the model on the processor alone.

You want it on your phone

PocketPal AI (iPhone and Android, MIT) and Atomic Chat (iPhone and Android, Apache 2.0) run models on the phone itself, and AnythingLLM has an Android app. Phones fit smaller models than a PC, so the answers are simpler.

You're on Windows and don't want to choose models

This is the case I built GreenCube for. It reads your graphics card and RAM and downloads the AI that fits, then chats, reads PDFs and photos, makes flashcards and generates pictures, offline. It costs €9.99 once and runs on Windows 10 and 11. There's more on what a Windows PC needs.

Will it be faster than LM Studio?

Most of these apps run the same open engine underneath, llama.cpp, so on the same PC with the same model the speed is set mostly by your hardware, not the app. We haven't timed the apps side by side, so I won't claim one is faster. What we did measure is GreenCube on 10 real PCs:

Your exact card is in Can my PC run AI? The biggest speed rule holds in every app: a model that fits whole on your graphics card is fast, and one that spills out of it is slow (we measured 6 times slower).

Questions

What is the best open-source alternative to LM Studio?

Jan is the closest: a desktop chat app that runs models itself, with a local OpenAI-compatible server, Apache 2.0, for Windows, Mac and Linux. Ollama (MIT) is the other main one, with a command line plus a chat window on Windows and Mac.

Is there an LM Studio alternative for Intel Macs?

Ollama. LM Studio and Jan need an Apple chip, but Ollama's docs list Intel Macs (x86) as supported, CPU only, on macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.

Is there an LM Studio alternative for Android?

LM Studio has no phone app, but PocketPal AI (Android and iPhone), Atomic Chat (Android and iPhone) and AnythingLLM's Android app run models on the phone itself.

Is LM Studio free?

Yes, even for work, but it's closed source. On Windows it needs a processor with AVX2; on Mac, an Apple chip and macOS 14 or newer.

Is there an LM Studio alternative that picks the model for me?

Yes, GreenCube (Windows 10 and 11, €9.99 once): it checks your graphics card and RAM and downloads the AI that fits, so there's no list of models. The free alternatives all let you pick one yourself, and expect you to.
