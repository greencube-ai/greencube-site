Can an Intel Core Ultra 5 laptop run AI? Measured: 14–18 tokens a second

By Hector Gras · 14 October 2026 · Leer en español

Yes. On a laptop with no graphics card (Intel Core Ultra 5 125U) and 16 GB of RAM, Gemma 4 E2B wrote 14–18 tokens a second. You read about 5, so answers come out faster than you can read them.

Where we have several runs we show the range: speeds move a little between runs on the same PC.

What it means

No graphics card? It still works

With no card, the AI runs on the processor. The smaller Gemma 4 E2B wrote 14–18 tokens a second. The bigger Gemma 4 E4B works too, at 8–10.

Pictures

Yes. One picture (512×512) took 2:02–2:38 min on the built-in Intel graphics, and 11:27–13:32 min on the processor only.

The test PC

  • Laptop
  • Graphics: built-in Intel graphics only
  • Graphics memory (VRAM): none (no separate graphics card)
  • RAM: 16 GB
  • Processor: Intel Core Ultra 5 125U, 14 threads

Other PCs we measured

  • NVIDIA RTX 2080 Ti, 11 GB: 65–71 tokens a second
  • AMD RX 5700 XT, 8 GB: 63–72 tokens a second
  • NVIDIA RTX 5060, 8 GB: 56 tokens a second
  • NVIDIA GTX 1660 Super, 6 GB: 35–38 tokens a second
  • NVIDIA GTX 1650, 4 GB: about 20 tokens a second
  • Intel Iris Xe only: 9.3 tokens a second
