Privacy First Lifetime AI Software: Choose the $9.99 Offline Option

By Hector Gras · 26 September 2026

We checked this
  • AI without a subscription: what actually exists in 2026, with checked prices

Lifetime AI software comes in two very different kinds. Some runs on your own computer, so a one-time price makes sense: there's no server bill behind it. The rest are cloud tools sold once through deal sites, where "lifetime" often means a monthly credit allowance that never ends rather than unlimited use. If privacy matters to you, the first kind is the one to look at; GreenCube is one, for €9.99 paid once.

I build GreenCube, so weigh my opinion with that in mind. Here's what the common one-time AI deals actually include, and what to check before you pay.

Why "lifetime" means different things

Running a cloud AI costs the seller money every time you use it. So when a cloud tool is sold for one payment, the seller usually limits how much you can use each month to keep the numbers working. Software that runs on your own PC has no such cost: your computer does the work, so there's nothing to meter.

ChatGPT, for what it's worth, has no lifetime plan at all. It's free with limits, or a monthly subscription.

One-time AI deals worth knowing

1min.AI (cloud, many models)

1min.AI is usually sold through deal marketplaces as one place to use several cloud AI models. The access normally comes as a monthly credit allowance, not open-ended use. The price depends on the marketplace and the day, so check the current listing rather than trusting a figure you read somewhere.

Narrower tools

  • Writecream writes marketing copy from templates, for freelancers who'd rather not pay monthly for a writing tool.
  • Airbrush makes AI images, for people who want a one-time cost instead of an image subscription.
  • Screpy monitors websites for SEO problems and sends reports.
  • Labrika bundles a mix of AI tools in one purchase, for buyers who want breadth over depth.
  • SheetMagic brings AI into spreadsheets, for people who do most of their work in them.

All of these run in the cloud, so the same question applies to each: is it unlimited, or a monthly allowance?

How to check a lifetime deal before you buy

  1. Read the exact wording on limits. "Unlimited" and "unlimited, with monthly credits" are different offers.
  2. Check how long the product has been around and whether it still ships updates.
  3. Find the refund window and who honours it, the maker or the marketplace.
  4. Check whether future versions are included or sold again later.
  5. If a team will use it, check the seat limit.

Marketplaces such as AppSumo, Dealify and StackSocial show buyer reviews and their own refund rules, which helps with the first three.

Does paying once actually save money?

Do the sum for your own case. Take what a subscription would cost you each month and divide the one-time price by it. A deal priced at ten times the monthly fee pays for itself after ten months, and only if you'd really have kept paying that long. If you usually cancel subscriptions after a few months, a lifetime deal isn't automatically the better buy.

It's a good idea when you use a tool often, when you work with documents you'd rather keep off the cloud, or when you simply want to stop paying monthly for something you rely on. It's a poor fit if you need the newest, strongest model for heavy reasoning, or if your team depends on shared cloud features. If you only open an AI now and then, a free plan is enough until your habits say otherwise (what ChatGPT's free plan allows).

Questions

Does ChatGPT have a lifetime subscription?

No. ChatGPT is free with limits or a monthly subscription, with no one-time plan.

Is there a free AI with unlimited use?

Not in the cloud: free plans cap use because every message costs the seller money. An AI on your own PC has no server bill, so free local apps like Ollama and LM Studio, and GreenCube at €9.99 once, have no message limits.

How much does a lifetime subscription to 1min.AI cost?

It depends on who's selling it and changes over time, so check the current listing. You usually get a monthly credit allowance rather than unlimited use.

What is the best AI software for free?

It depends on the job, and most free cloud plans have usage caps. If you use AI a lot, paying once for an app on your PC, like GreenCube, can cost less over time than a subscription.
