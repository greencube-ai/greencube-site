Shortlist: what each lifetime deal actually includes

Lifetime AI deals fall into two camps: software that runs on your machine and never touches the cloud, and cloud suites sold through marketplaces with a one-time price tag but ongoing server costs behind them. That difference matters more than the sticker price.

GreenCube is a desktop AI assistant that runs entirely offline on Windows. You pick a model once during setup, and after that your chats and documents never leave your PC. The license is a single payment with a 14-day refund window, and there is no subscription behind it.

1min.AI is typically sold through deal marketplaces as a multi-model cloud platform. It bundles access to several AI models under one purchase, but that access is usually metered with monthly credit allowances rather than open-ended use, since running many cloud models costs the seller money every month.

The rest of the shortlist covers narrower jobs:

  • Writecream focuses on writing templates and marketing copy, a fit for freelancers who want a fixed-price alternative to a subscription writing tool.
  • Airbrush is built around AI image generation, useful for creatives who want a one-time cost instead of a recurring image-generation subscription.
  • Screpy handles SEO monitoring and site reports, aimed at site owners who want ongoing audits without a monthly fee.
  • Labrika bundles a variety of AI tools into one purchase, appealing to buyers who want breadth over depth.
  • SheetMagic automates spreadsheet-driven content and data workflows, a fit for anyone who lives inside spreadsheets.

The one thing to check before buying any of the cloud entries above: whether "lifetime" means unlimited generation or a monthly credit refresh dressed up as unlimited. That wording decides whether the deal actually saves you money over a few years.

How to check a lifetime AI deal before you buy

A lifetime price tag only pays off if the product sticks around and the terms hold up. Before buying, run through this list.

  1. Check how long the product has existed and whether it still ships updates.
  2. Read the exact wording around usage limits: "unlimited" and "unlimited with monthly credits" are not the same offer.
  3. Confirm the refund window and whether it is enforced by the seller or a marketplace.
  4. Look for a public roadmap or changelog that shows active development.
  5. Check seat limits if you plan to use the tool with a team.
  6. Confirm whether future major updates are included or sold separately.

To see if the math works, compare the one-time price against a subscription over time. Say a cloud AI subscription costs $20 a month. A lifetime deal priced at $199 breaks even after roughly ten months of use, and every month after that is free. If you canceled a subscription within a year anyway, the lifetime deal is not automatically the better buy.

Pro Tip: Favor lifetime deals sold through established marketplaces such as AppSumo, Dealify, or StackSocial, since they publish clear refund policies and let you read buyer feedback before you commit.

Author perspective: when a lifetime AI buy is a smart move

A lifetime AI purchase makes sense when you use a tool often enough that a subscription would add up, when you handle sensitive documents and want them off the cloud, or when you simply want to stop paying monthly for something you already rely on. It makes less sense if you need the newest frontier model for heavy reasoning work or you depend on cloud collaboration features with a team.

If privacy is your priority, try GreenCube first. If you juggle several AI tools already, check the multi-model lifetime bundles on marketplaces before committing to separate subscriptions. If you only open an AI tool once in a while, wait and use a free option until your habits justify a purchase.

FAQ

Does ChatGPT have a lifetime subscription?

No. ChatGPT is sold on recurring monthly plans, not as a one-time lifetime purchase. Buyers looking for a true one-time cost need to look at dedicated lifetime-deal software instead.

Is there a free AI with unlimited use?

Several AI tools offer free tiers, but they typically cap usage, features, or model quality compared to paid versions. A one-time purchase like GreenCube or a marketplace lifetime deal is aimed at people who have outgrown those free limits.

How much does a lifetime subscription to 1min.AI cost?

1min.AI's lifetime pricing is set by the marketplace running the deal at any given time rather than a single fixed price, so it varies. Check the current listing on the marketplace itself before assuming a specific figure.

What is the best AI software for free?

There is no single best free AI tool since it depends on whether you need writing help, image generation, or document analysis, and free tiers usually come with usage caps. If you plan to rely on AI daily, a fixed-price option such as GreenCube can cost less over time than staying on a free tier that eventually pushes you toward a paid subscription.
