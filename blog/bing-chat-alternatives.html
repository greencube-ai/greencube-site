Bing Chat Alternatives: Keep Chats Offline or Choose Cloud

By Hector Gras · 11 October 2026

Bing Chat doesn't exist under that name any more: Microsoft renamed it Copilot in late 2023, and it's the same assistant, now in Windows, Edge, the Copilot app and Microsoft 365. If you want something other than Copilot, the main cloud alternatives are ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity. If you'd rather your chats didn't go to any company's server, you need an AI that runs on your own PC, such as the free LM Studio or GreenCube for €9.99 once.

I build GreenCube, so weigh my opinion with that in mind. Below are the alternatives, what each is good at, and how to pick.

What happened to Bing Chat

Bing Chat launched in early 2023 as a chatbot inside Bing search, running on OpenAI's GPT-4. Later that year Microsoft folded it into Copilot, its assistant across Windows and Office. So if you liked Bing Chat, the first thing to try is simply Copilot: it still searches the web as it answers, and it has a free version.

Is it the same as ChatGPT? No. Both have used OpenAI's models, but Copilot is Microsoft's product, with Bing search and Microsoft 365 built in, while ChatGPT is OpenAI's own app.

The cloud alternatives

ChatGPT

The most widely used general assistant, good at writing, explaining and brainstorming, with a free plan and paid plans. Your chats are kept on OpenAI's servers. See ChatGPT alternatives and ChatGPT's free limits.

Gemini

Google's assistant, which used to be called Bard. It's the natural pick if your work lives in Gmail, Docs and Google search. More in Gemini alternatives.

Claude

Anthropic's assistant, strong on long documents and careful writing. Free and paid plans, all in the cloud. See Claude alternatives.

Perplexity

The closest thing to what Bing Chat set out to be: a search engine that answers in sentences and shows its sources. Good for quick, current questions. See Perplexity alternatives.

Jasper, YouChat and Chatsonic

Older lists of Bing Chat alternatives often name these. Jasper is aimed at marketing teams writing copy, and it's sold by subscription. YouChat is the chat side of the You.com search engine, and Chatsonic is Writesonic's chatbot for writing with web results. They're niche picks next to the four above.

The offline alternative

Every tool above runs on someone else's servers and needs a connection. An offline AI is different: the model sits on your own disk, so you can switch the Wi-Fi off and keep going, and your chats stay on your computer.

GreenCube is a Windows app that does this without setup. It checks your PC and picks the model that fits: Cube 2B on smaller PCs, Cube 4B on PCs with a graphics card, both built on Google's Gemma. It needs the internet once, to sign in with Google or Microsoft (only to check you paid; it never sees your chats or files) and to download the AI. After that it works offline, and only goes online for updates or if you switch on "Online" for web lookups. It costs €9.99 once, with a 15-day refund.

How to pick

If you want current answers with sources, stay in the cloud: Copilot or Perplexity. If you use Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace all day, use the assistant built into it. If you want the strongest general model for hard problems, try ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini on your own questions and keep the one you like.

If privacy or a bad connection is the reason you're looking, go local. Be honest with yourself about the trade: an AI that fits on a laptop is less clever than the cloud ones on the hardest questions, doesn't know today's news unless it can search, and runs only as fast as your PC. On the PCs we tested, GreenCube wrote 5–7 tokens a second on an 8 GB laptop (about reading speed), 14–18 on a 16 GB laptop and 37–76 on most gaming PCs with a 6 GB or bigger card.

What not to type into a cloud chatbot

Whichever cloud assistant you use, keep passwords, bank and card numbers, medical records and other people's private details out of it, unless you've read how that company stores and uses chats. If you need an AI's help with that kind of material, that's exactly the case for one that runs on your own machine. More in is ChatGPT private?

Questions

What replaced Bing Chat?

Copilot. Microsoft renamed it in late 2023; it's the same assistant, now in Windows, Edge, the Copilot app and Microsoft 365, and it still searches the web as it answers.

Which is the best free AI chat option?

Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity all have free plans with limits, so try a few on your own questions. If you'd rather your chats stayed on your PC, free local apps like LM Studio, Ollama, Jan and GPT4All work offline once you've set up a model.

What shouldn't you share with a cloud-based AI chatbot?

Passwords, bank and card numbers, medical records and other people's private details, unless you've read how that company stores and uses chats. For that kind of thing, an AI on your own machine is the safer place.

Is Bing Chat the same as ChatGPT?

No. Both have used OpenAI's models, but Copilot (Bing Chat's new name) is Microsoft's product with Bing search and Microsoft 365 built in, while ChatGPT is OpenAI's own app.

Do I need an internet connection to use a local AI assistant like GreenCube?

Only at the start. GreenCube needs internet once to sign in (just to check you paid) and to download the AI, then works offline, going online only for updates or if you switch on Online for web lookups.
