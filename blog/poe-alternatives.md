The right Poe alternative depends on what you used Poe for. For many models in one app, Msty and Jan mix online models with models that run on your own computer. For one strong assistant, go straight to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. And if you want nothing to leave your PC, use a local app like LM Studio, Jan, Ollama or GreenCube.

Poe, run by Quora, is handy: lots of AI bots in one place. The catch is in its own privacy policy, which says the model providers and bot developers behind those bots may receive your chats, and some may store them to train their models. I build GreenCube, one of the local apps below, so weigh my opinion with that in mind.

## What Poe's privacy policy says about your chats

From Poe's privacy policy, last updated 30 April 2026 ([Poe](https://poe.com/privacy)):

- Its AI model providers and developers "may receive details about your interactions with bots and apps on Poe (including the contents of your chats and photos or documents you upload)".
- Developers that build bots through APIs "may view and store your chats on their servers to train their models".
- Your account details aren't shared with model providers or developers, unless you chat with other users in a group chat.
- If you use Poe's memory feature, Poe will share that memory with the other model providers you talk to.

That's the deal with any one-app-for-every-bot service. Each bot is run by someone else, and your messages go to them. That's fine for plenty of questions, but think twice before pasting in school work that isn't yours, private notes or anything personal.

We couldn't read Poe's plan prices or points system on its own pages when we checked, so we don't quote them here. They're on [Poe's subscribe page](https://poe.com/subscribe).

## The alternatives, by what you used Poe for

### Many models in one app

**Msty Studio** lets you "chat with local and online models" in one app, running the local ones through Ollama, llama.cpp or MLX. Studio Free is "$0 Forever", and the paid Aurum plan is $149 per user per year, or $349 once ([Msty](https://msty.ai/pricing/)).

**Jan** is free and open source and lets you "choose from open models or plug in your favorite online models", naming ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, Mistral, Qwen, DeepSeek, Gemma and Kimi ([Jan](https://jan.ai)).

Both are the closest thing to Poe's mix of models. Keep in mind that in either app, an online model still sends your chat to its company; only the local ones keep it on your machine.

### One strong assistant, straight from the maker

Using ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini directly means one company's models instead of a marketplace of bots, and their rules are easy to check. With a free personal account, ChatGPT trains on your chats by default (you can switch it off), Claude only if you allow it, and Gemini while Keep Activity is on. Each one with its source is in [the most private AI assistant, 10 checked](https://blog.greencube.app/blog/private-ai-assistant).

### Your chats stay on your computer

A local app downloads a model once and runs it on your own graphics card or processor, so no one else gets your chats and it works offline. **LM Studio**, **Jan** and **Ollama** are free and let you pick the models. **GreenCube** (Windows, €9.99 once) picks the model for your PC and also reads PDFs and photos and makes pictures. A longer list is in [12 offline AI apps, checked](https://blog.greencube.app/blog/best-offline-ai-chatbot).

## FAQ

### What is Poe?

A chat app from Quora where you talk to many AI bots in one place, including models from OpenAI, Anthropic and DeepSeek and thousands of bots made by other people. It's on the web, Windows, Mac, iPhone and Android.

### Does Poe share my chats?

Yes. Poe's privacy policy (updated 30 April 2026) says the companies and developers behind its bots may get your chats and uploaded files, and developers who build bots through APIs may view and store your chats on their servers to train their models.

### What is the best Poe alternative for using many AI models in one app?

Msty and Jan both let you use online models and models on your own computer in one app. Jan is free and open source; Msty Studio has a free plan and paid Aurum plans at $149 a year or $349 once. Using an online model in either still sends your chat to that model's company.

### Is there a Poe alternative that keeps my chats private?

An app that runs the AI on your own PC, like LM Studio, Jan, Ollama or GreenCube. After the first download your chats stay on your computer and it works offline.
