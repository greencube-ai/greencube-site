Keep Files Local for $9.99: Gemini vs GreenCube for Privacy First Users

By Hector Gras · 19 September 2026

We checked this
  • Which AI doesn't train on your chats? We checked 9 (October 2026)
  • AI to summarize your notes: which to pick for limits and privacy (2026)

Gemini is the more capable AI, with Google Search built in and deep ties to Gmail, Docs and Android. GreenCube is the better fit if you want your files to stay on your own Windows PC: it runs offline, saves your chats on your computer, and costs €9.99 (US$9.99) once instead of a monthly plan. Interestingly, GreenCube's AI is built on Gemma, Google's own open model family.

I build GreenCube, so weigh my opinion with that in mind. Below is an honest side by side, what your PC needs, how setup works and who each one suits.

Cloud Gemini and local Gemma

Gemini runs in Google's data centres. Whatever you type, and any file you upload, goes to Google, a very large model answers, and the chat is kept with your Google account according to your activity settings. Google's own privacy notice for Gemini says some conversations may be read by human reviewers, and advises against entering anything confidential.

Google also publishes a smaller family of open models called Gemma, built from the same research as Gemini, that anyone can download and run on their own hardware. GreenCube uses them: it installs Cube 2B on smaller PCs and Cube 4B on PCs with a graphics card, both built on Gemma, and runs them on your computer. So it's a much smaller model than Gemini, but one that never needs to send anything to a server.

Where Gemini is better

Gemini is far bigger than anything that fits in a laptop, so it handles hard reasoning, tricky code and niche facts better. Because it sits on top of Google Search, it's good at current events and quick lookups. If your life already runs on Gmail, Google Docs and an Android phone, having the AI inside those apps saves a lot of copying and pasting, and the paid plan also comes with extra Google storage.

Where GreenCube is the better fit

Your files stay with you. Contracts, medical letters, coursework and personal notes can be read and summarised without being uploaded anywhere. You sign in once (Google or Microsoft, only to check you paid; it never sees your chats or files), it downloads the AI, and from then on it only goes online for updates or if you turn on Online for web lookups.

It also works anywhere and has no meter. There's no message cap, no credits and no monthly fee, and it keeps working on a plane or with the router off. Besides chat, it makes slide decks that save as PowerPoint, quizzes, flashcards, labelled diagrams and pictures, all on your PC.

What your PC needs

Gemini only needs a browser or a phone. GreenCube needs a Windows 10 or 11 PC with about 8 GB of memory or more. It sorts PCs into four levels so you know what to expect: Seed (about 8 GB, no graphics card; everything works, just slower), Sprout (16 GB with no card, or 8 GB with a card), Bloom (a graphics card with 8 GB, or a 4 to 6 GB card with 16 GB of memory) and Thrive (an 8 GB or bigger card and 24 GB or more of memory). On the PCs we tested, it wrote 5 to 7 tokens a second on an 8 GB laptop (about reading speed), 14 to 18 on a 16 GB laptop, and 37 to 76 on most gaming PCs with a 6 GB or bigger card. Pictures need 12 GB of memory.

You can check your PC here, and Gemma 4's requirements go into more detail.

Setting up GreenCube

  1. Download and install it on Windows 10 or 11.
  2. Sign in once with Google or Microsoft, so it can check that you paid.
  3. Let it check your PC and download the model that fits. This is the only big download.
  4. Switch the Wi-Fi off and try a chat. It should work exactly the same.

If it isn't for you, there's a 15-day refund: email support@greencube.app.

Who should pick which

Pick Gemini if you need the strongest model, want Google Search and current news in your answers, live in Gmail and Google Docs, or mostly use AI on a phone.

Pick GreenCube if you use a Windows PC and want your files kept on it, often work offline, want no limits, or would rather pay €9.99 once than $19.99 a month. Using both is sensible too: Gemini for searches and hard problems, GreenCube for anything private.

Questions

Is GreenCube really free of subscriptions?

Yes. GreenCube costs €9.99 (US$9.99) once, with everything included and no recurring fee. There's a 15-day refund if it doesn't suit your PC.

Does GreenCube work without internet?

Yes, after setup. It needs the internet once, to sign in with Google or Microsoft (only to check you paid) and to download the AI. After that it only goes online for updates or if you switch Online on for web lookups.

Do I have to pick a GreenCube model?

No. GreenCube checks your PC and picks for you: Cube 2B on smaller PCs and Cube 4B on PCs with a graphics card, both built on Google's Gemma.

Can Gemini or other cloud models still beat GreenCube?

Yes. Gemini is far bigger, so it's better on hard reasoning, tricky code, niche facts and current news. GreenCube trades that for offline use, chats kept on your PC and a one-time price.

Does GreenCube run on Mac?

No. GreenCube is for Windows 10 and 11 only.
