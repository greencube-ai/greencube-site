What Humata is, and why people look for alternatives

Humata is a web app where you upload PDFs and other files and ask questions about them; its answers link back to the part of your file they come from (Humata). People usually look for an alternative for one of three reasons: the free plan runs out (60 pages a month, up to 10 answers), they don't want a monthly bill, or they'd rather not upload their documents to a company's cloud. Which one fits you?

You want it free and don't mind the cloud: NotebookLM

Up to 50 sources per notebook, answers that cite the part of your files they come from, and Google says it doesn't train on your uploads unless you rate an answer. You need internet and a Google account, and your files go to Google.

You need answers that point to the exact page

Humata, NotebookLM, Adobe Acrobat's AI Assistant and ChatPDF all say their answers link back to the source in your document. That matters for essays and research, where you have to check and quote. Acrobat's AI comes with a paid plan; NotebookLM is free.

You don't want to upload your files, or you hit page limits

An app that runs the AI on your own computer reads PDFs from your disk, so nothing is uploaded and there's no page limit; the only cost is your computer's time. GreenCube (Windows, €9.99 once) picks the AI that fits your PC by itself. LM Studio, Jan, GPT4All and AnythingLLM are free; there you choose a model yourself. You're comparing monthly costs

Humata Expert is $9.99 a month: $119.88 a year. Acrobat Studio is $299.88 a year. ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro are $20 a month. A local app is free or paid once; GreenCube is €9.99 once.

What to watch for, whichever you pick

  • Check what matters. Every AI here can misread a document. In our test, GreenCube got 1 question in 12 wrong. Check anything you'll quote or be graded on against the PDF.
  • Read the limits before a deadline. Free plans count pages, documents or messages differently: Humata counts pages a month, ChatPDF documents a day, Gemini files per message.
  • Someone else's documents. If the PDF is your school's, your employer's or a client's, check whether you're allowed to upload it to a cloud service first.

How we checked

Questions

Is Humata AI free?

There's a free plan with 60 pages a month and up to 10 answers. Expert costs $9.99 a month for 500 pages (then $0.02 a page), and Team $49 per user a month.

What is the best free alternative to Humata?

In the cloud, NotebookLM: free, up to 50 sources per notebook, answers that cite your sources, and Google says it doesn't train on your uploads unless you send feedback. If you don't want to upload your files anywhere, LM Studio, Jan, GPT4All and AnythingLLM are free apps that read PDFs on your own computer.

Does Humata train on my documents?

Humata says it doesn't use users' data to train its AI models. Your files are stored in its cloud (it names Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud and Supabase), and document data used by its model isn't kept beyond 30 days.

Is there a Humata alternative that works offline?

Yes: an app that runs the AI on your own computer. GreenCube (Windows, €9.99 once) and the free apps LM Studio, Jan, GPT4All and AnythingLLM read PDFs without uploading them. Speed depends on your PC.

Which alternatives cite the page an answer comes from?

Humata, NotebookLM, Adobe Acrobat AI Assistant and ChatPDF all say their answers point back to the source in your document.
