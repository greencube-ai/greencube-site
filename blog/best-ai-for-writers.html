Most private AI writing assistant: training, retention and confidential documents (2026)

By Hector Gras · 25 August 2026 · updated 23 October 2026

Short answer: the most private AI writing assistant is one that runs on your own PC: your drafts never reach a company, so nothing can be trained on, kept or reviewed. In the cloud, privacy depends on the plan and your settings: on a free personal account 5 of 9 popular AIs train on your text by default, retention ranges from 30 days to 5 years, and switching training off doesn't cover feedback or human review.

What "private" means for a writing assistant

  • Training: is your text used to train the model? Most free plans say yes by default.
  • Retention: how long is your text kept, even if it isn't used for training?
  • Human review: can people at the company read some of it?
  • Where it's processed: on the company's servers, or on your own machine?

Confidential documents: client work, contracts, unpublished writing

Check the rules first

Your contract, your client or your publisher may forbid putting their text into a cloud service at all. That decides more than any setting.

Business and work accounts

OpenAI doesn't train on Business, Enterprise or API data by default (checked), and work and school accounts are usually left out of training on the big services. The text is still processed and stored on the company's servers.

Keep it on your machine

An AI that runs on your own PC reads and writes locally: no upload, no retention, no reviewers. GreenCube drafts emails, essays and documents and makes PowerPoint files, offline, €9.99 once; free apps like LM Studio, Jan and GPT4All do it too if you're happy choosing a model.

Writing assistant or general chatbot?

For privacy the question isn't the label but where the text goes: a "writing assistant" built on a cloud model sends your text to that model's servers just like a chatbot does. Compare the four points above for whichever tool you use. For quality, the biggest cloud models are still stronger at the hardest writing; a model that fits on a PC handles emails, essays, summaries and rewrites well.

How fast is a private AI on your own PC?

Measured with GreenCube on 10 real PCs: 5–7 tokens a second on an 8 GB laptop (about reading speed), 14–18 on a 16 GB laptop, 37–76 on most gaming PCs. Check yours: Can my PC run AI? More on private assistants: the most private AI assistant.

Questions

Which AI writing assistant has the best privacy?

One that runs on your own computer, because your drafts never reach a company. Among cloud assistants on free personal accounts, NotebookLM says it doesn't train on uploads unless you send feedback and Claude only trains if you allow it; ChatGPT, Perplexity, Mistral, DeepSeek and Meta AI train by default unless you switch it off.

Which AI writing tools have data retention controls?

ChatGPT's Temporary Chats aren't used for training and are deleted after 30 days. Gemini keeps chats 72 hours after you turn Keep Activity off, but keeps human-reviewed chats up to 3 years. Claude keeps chats up to 5 years if you allow training. Perplexity's switch only stops future data.

Is it safe to use AI for confidential client documents?

Check your contract and your client's rules first. Business, Enterprise and API plans from OpenAI aren't trained on by default, and work accounts are usually excluded on the big services, but the text is still processed on the company's servers. An AI that runs on your own PC keeps the document on your machine.

What is the most private AI for students' writing?

A school account (usually excluded from training) or an AI that runs on your own PC. On free personal accounts most cloud AIs train on what you type by default; switch it off before pasting anything, and don't rate sensitive chats.

Does turning off training make an AI writing assistant private?

Not completely. Feedback (thumbs up or down) can still send the whole conversation for training at OpenAI and Mistral, human reviewers may read some chats, and data already used isn't removed.
