ChatGPT & your data

Does ChatGPT train
on your chats?

Short answer: on the personal plans, yes, unless you turn it off.

Which plans

Who gets trained on.

Free, Go, Plus, ProYes, by default. You can turn it off.
Temporary ChatNo. And it isn’t kept in your history.
Business, Enterprise, APINo, not by default.

Source: OpenAI Help Center, Data Controls.

Turn it off

Three steps. Takes a minute.

  1. 1

    Open Settings

    Click your profile picture, then Settings.

  2. 2

    Data Controls

    Go to the Data Controls section.

  3. 3

    Switch it off

    Turn off “Improve the model for everyone”.

It stops future chats from being used for training. It doesn’t delete what was already used, and your chats are still stored on OpenAI’s servers.

Try it

What the switch actually does.

On: your chats can be used to train ChatGPT.

Who else

Who can see your chats?

People can read them

OpenAI says authorised staff and outside contractors can access conversations, for example for support, abuse checks and legal reasons.

Deleted isn’t always deleted

In 2025, during the New York Times lawsuit, a court ordered OpenAI to keep chats people had deleted. That mostly ended in October 2025, but chats saved before then can still be kept.

It’s not just OpenAI

Gemini, Claude, Copilot and Meta AI all have their own rules and switches. The shape is the same: if the AI runs on someone else’s computer, your words go there.

Sources: OpenAI privacy policy · the October 2025 order.

Questions

Quick answers.

Does ChatGPT train on my chats?

On Free, Go, Plus and Pro, yes by default, unless you turn off “Improve the model for everyone” in Settings, Data Controls. Business, Enterprise and the API are not trained on by default.

Does Temporary Chat get used for training?

No. Temporary Chats aren’t used for training and don’t appear in your history.

Are deleted ChatGPT chats really deleted?

Usually, yes. But in 2025 a court ordered OpenAI to keep deleted chats; that mostly ended in October 2025, and chats saved before then can still be kept.

How do I keep my chats completely private?

Use an AI that runs on your own computer, like GreenCube. Then there’s no server holding your chats at all.
