Identity and mission

Our mission is to restore privacy to everyone.

Not just the people who know which settings to change. Everyone.

What GreenCube is

An AI that lives on your own laptop.

What it is

An app you install on your laptop, the same way you install a game.

How it works

Your laptop does the thinking. Normally an AI runs on a company's computer far away, so everything you type has to travel there. This one does not.

Why it matters

It works with no internet. And your words stay on your laptop, so nobody can read them.

Who we build for

Everyday people who are tired of accepting cookies.

Not engineers. Not people who read privacy policies for fun. The people who click Accept All because there is no other button. They never agreed to have their life sold. They just ran out of ways to say no.

Humanity deserves full ownership of their data. Unless they explicitly choose to share it.
