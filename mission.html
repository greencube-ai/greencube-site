Principles & philosophy

Four things every decision runs through.

Not wall art. These are the arguments we actually have, written down so they stay settled.

01

First principles

Don't listen to "it's impossible." If it doesn't break the laws of physics, it can be done — and usually the only thing in the way is that nobody has bothered yet.

02

The user

The user is who we work for. Treat them as family. Every single decision has to be made entirely in their best interest — including the ones that cost us money.

03

Use the product

Use GreenCube every day. You can't build for someone you don't sit next to, and the fastest way to understand what a user needs is to need it yourself.

04

Buy once, own forever

You should own your software, your music, your movies. No monthly subscriptions, no selling your data, no getting quietly steered by an algorithm you never asked for.
