Questions people ask before buying.

Is it really one payment?

Yes. €9.99 once and it’s yours. No subscription, no renewal, no second charge.

Will it run on my PC?

If it runs Windows 10 or 11 and has 8 GB of RAM or more, yes. A graphics card makes it fast; without one it’s slower, but it works. Check your PC in two questions.

Do I need internet?

Once, to sign in and download the AI. After that it works with no internet: on a plane, in a blackout, anywhere. It only goes online for updates, or if you switch Online on.

Why do I need an account?

Only to check that you paid. It never sees your chats, files or images.

Is it as smart as ChatGPT?

No. The biggest cloud AIs are smarter. GreenCube has no limits, works offline and is yours. That’s the trade.

What if it’s not for me?

Email support@greencube.app within 14 days and we’ll refund you.

A note from Hector

Hi, I'm Hector. I built GreenCube because I was tired of AI telling me I'd hit my limit and had to wait hours to use it again. You could run AI on your own computer instead, but that meant choosing a model and setting it all up yourself, and most people have no idea where to start. So I made GreenCube: you install it, and it works. No limits, nothing to set up.

Every computer is different, and I can't know how GreenCube will run on yours. I don't want anyone stuck with something that doesn't work for them. Downloads usually can't be refunded, but I'd rather you were happy: if it isn't right for you within 14 days, email me, tell me what happened, and I'll refund you in full.
